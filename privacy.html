Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MikeCraft (“MikeCraft,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website, dashboard, agents, and related services (the “Service”).

1. Scope

MikeCraft helps create and publish Minecraft tutorial content. Depending on your setup, the Service may run locally on your computer and/or on your own servers (for example, a self-hosted worker machine).

2. Information we process

Depending on how you configure the Service, we may process:

  • Account and auth data you connect, such as OAuth tokens for YouTube or TikTok;
  • Project content, including titles, scripts, captions, thumbnails, audio, video files, and metadata;
  • Configuration data, such as AI model choices, publish mode, and pipeline settings;
  • Operational logs, such as job status, error messages, and agent activity needed to run productions;
  • Technical data, such as IP addresses and basic server logs if you host a public web endpoint.

We do not require you to submit special-category personal data. Please do not put sensitive personal information into scripts or prompts.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Operate research, scripting, voiceover, editing, SEO, and publishing workflows;
  • Authenticate to platforms you connect and upload content at your direction;
  • Maintain project history, approvals, and settings;
  • Improve reliability, debug failures, and secure the Service;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. AI providers

If you enable cloud AI (for example OpenAI) or local models (for example Ollama), prompts and related content may be sent to those providers under your own API keys and their privacy terms. MikeCraft does not sell your content.

5. Third-party platforms

When you connect YouTube, TikTok, or similar services, those platforms receive data necessary to authenticate and publish. Their privacy policies govern their handling of that data.

6. Storage and retention

Project files, databases, tokens, and logs are typically stored on systems you control (local machine and/or your servers). Retain or delete them according to your own operational needs. Revoke OAuth access in YouTube/TikTok settings when you no longer want MikeCraft connected.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by subprocessors you configure (hosting, AI, TTS, storage) solely to provide the Service. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

8. Security

We recommend storing secrets in environment variables or private secret files, restricting machine access, and rotating tokens regularly. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. Do not use MikeCraft to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

You may disconnect third-party integrations, delete local project data, and stop using the Service at any time. For requests related to personal data processed in a hosted deployment you control, contact the operator of that deployment.

11. International users

If you access the Service from outside the country where your systems are hosted, your information may be processed in that host location.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the maintainer of your MikeCraft deployment or repository.
